About Us

Electroreck is the society of passionate electrical engineering students at NIT Kurukshetra. We plan and run intra and inter-college technical events and workshops built around core electrical and robotics concepts.

Every year we organise a series of events during TECHSPARDHA, the technical fest of NIT Kurukshetra.

Through our initiatives we build a collaborative, innovative space where students explore, learn and grow, tackling real-world challenges along the way. Join Electroreck to be part of a dynamic team that cares about excellence and learning that counts.
